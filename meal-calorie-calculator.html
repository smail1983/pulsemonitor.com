How to Calculate Calories in a Meal

Add every food or drink in your meal as a separate row, along with its calories and macronutrients per serving. Adjust the quantity for how many servings you had, and the totals update automatically.

Understanding Calories and Macronutrients

Calories measure the energy in food. Protein, carbohydrates, and fat — the three macronutrients — each contribute calories in different amounts, and tracking them alongside total calories can give a fuller picture of a meal.

Why Serving Size Matters

Nutrition information on labels is usually listed per serving, not per package. Adjusting the quantity field to match how much you actually ate helps keep your totals accurate.

How to Read a Nutrition Label

Most nutrition labels list calories and macronutrients per serving near the top, with the serving size stated just above. Check the serving size first, then match your portion to it before entering values here.
