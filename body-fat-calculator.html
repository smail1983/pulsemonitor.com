What Is Body Fat Percentage?

Body fat percentage is the proportion of your total body weight that comes from fat, as opposed to muscle, bone, organs, and water. Unlike BMI, it gives a more direct sense of body composition.

How Is Body Fat Calculated Here?

This calculator uses the U.S. Navy circumference method, which estimates body fat from your height, neck, and waist measurements (plus hip measurement for women). It's a widely used field method that doesn't require special equipment.

How to Measure Correctly

Use a flexible tape measure and keep it snug but not tight against the skin. Measure your neck just below the larynx, your waist at the narrowest point (or at the navel if there is no natural narrowing), and, for women, your hips at the widest point.

Limitations

The circumference method is an estimate, not a direct measurement. Clinical methods such as DEXA scans or hydrostatic weighing are more precise but require specialized equipment. Measurement technique also affects accuracy, so try to measure consistently each time.
